# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

This fork is a professional space for terminal UI, agent-interface, and automation engineering. Contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to communicate respectfully, review ideas in good faith, and make room for differing technical perspectives and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

Use constructive, evidence-based feedback. Respect privacy, intellectual property, accessibility, and the security of third-party systems. Keep discussion focused on the work, state assumptions and limitations, and help make interface decisions understandable to users and operators.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, and instructions intended to bypass safeguards are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a conduct concern through the private contact route in [SUPPORT.md](./SUPPORT.md). Maintainers will review credible reports and may request a correction, remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate action. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-related communication.
